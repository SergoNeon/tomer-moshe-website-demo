Lease agreements: why a generic online template may not be enough

A lease should reflect the specific property, the parties and the actual risks of the arrangement.

Security and guarantees

Define what security is provided, when it may be used and how it is returned at the end of the lease.

Condition and repairs

Document the condition of the property and allocate responsibility for maintenance, damage and insurance.

Termination and handover

Renewal, early termination, notice and vacating terms should be clear in advance to reduce disputes.
