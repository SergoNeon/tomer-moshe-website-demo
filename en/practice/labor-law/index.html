What can employment-law support include?

It may include ongoing advice, employer liability, employee compensation, disputes and preparation for or management of employment proceedings.

Which documents are useful when reviewing an employment matter?

Employment agreements, payslips, notices, correspondence and records relating to working time or termination may be relevant.
