FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should be clear before an agreement is signed?

The agreement should clearly address the parties’ obligations, payment, timing, termination, safeguards and the consequences of a breach.

Which documents may be needed before litigation?

Depending on the matter, demand letters, statements of claim or defence, applications, affidavits and supporting materials may be required.
