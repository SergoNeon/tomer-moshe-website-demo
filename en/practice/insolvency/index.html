Frequently asked questions

Which matters fall within insolvency work?

The practice includes debt arrangements, insolvency proceedings, liquidation and receivership, and matters concerning corporate rehabilitation where relevant.

What is useful to prepare for an initial review of a debt situation?

A list of debts and creditors, enforcement files, existing decisions and basic income and expense information can help establish the initial picture.
