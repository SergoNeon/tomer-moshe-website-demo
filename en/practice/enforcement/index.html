FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does representation of a debtor differ from representation of a creditor?

Debtor representation commonly focuses on collection proceedings, objections and debt arrangements, while creditor representation focuses on enforcing rights and collecting debts under appropriate instruments.

Which documents are important when reviewing an enforcement file?

Judgments or decisions, cheques or promissory notes, enforcement-file documents, notices and payment records may be important to the review.
