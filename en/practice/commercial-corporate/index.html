Frequently asked questions

What can ongoing legal support for a company include?

It may include regulatory advice, commercial documents and agreements, ongoing support and legal review of transactions or corporate changes.

When should a commercial transaction be legally reviewed?

Before taking on a material obligation, signing an agreement or completing a significant corporate step, the documents, risks and obligations should be assessed.
