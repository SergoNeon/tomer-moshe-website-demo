What types of matters fall under insurance and torts?

The practice includes bodily injury, accidents and other matters where responsibility and the extent of damage need to be assessed.

What should be kept after an incident that caused injury or damage?

Depending on the matter, medical records, incident and insurance details, photographs, reports and relevant correspondence may be useful.
